\section{Numerical results}
\label{sec:results}

We implemented the dynamics of Definition~\ref{def:moves} as a stochastic
process on directed graphs (code in Appendix~\ref{app:code}; the
implementation currently realizes sprouts and $\Lambda^2_1$ fills, i.e.\
tower level $d \le 2$). At each tick a sprout occurs with probability
$p_{\mathrm{sprout}}$, otherwise a uniformly random unfilled inner horn is
filled. Dependencies follow Definition~\ref{def:causalorder}: a sprout at
$v$ depends on the event that created $v$; a fill of the horn
$(u \to v \to w)$ depends on the events that created its two edges. The
entropy of Definition~\ref{def:entropy} is computed exactly by dynamic
programming over downsets of the event poset, which limits runs to
$\approx 20$ events (the \#P wall).

\begin{figure}[ht]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=0.85\textwidth]{figures/entropy_growth.png}
  \caption{Causal-past entropy $S(t)$ (mean over six seeds, 18 events) for
  three sprout probabilities. Entropy is monotone in every run and ordered
  by the sprout fraction at every time: sprouts create causally independent
  events (parallelism), fills create dependencies (sequentialization).}
  \label{fig:entropy}
\end{figure}

Three observations, with their status:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Monotonicity} (supports Lemma~\ref{lem:monotone}): $S(t)$
        is strictly increasing in every run.
  \item \textbf{Ratio ordering}: $S(t)$ is pointwise increasing in
        $p_{\mathrm{sprout}}$. Interpretation: expansion produces entropy;
        equilibration (filling) suppresses its growth. This links the
        entropy functional to the expansion dynamics and is the empirical
        hook for treating the dynamics as an entropic gradient flow.
        \gap{significance analysis: more seeds, error bars, and a test of
        pointwise ordering}
  \item \textbf{No visible deceleration}: growth is roughly linear at this
        scale, as expected --- 18 events barely populates tower level
        $d = 3$, so the mechanism of Section~\ref{sec:tower} cannot yet
        bite. \gap{sampling-based estimator for $\Ext$ (e.g.\ a
        Karzanov--Khachiyan-style Markov chain) to reach $10^2$--$10^3$
        events, plus implementation of $d \ge 3$ horn filling, then test
        for power-law $N(t)$ and for entropy deceleration}
\end{enumerate}
