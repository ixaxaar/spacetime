\section{The budget triangle}
\label{app:budget}

Let the total move rate of any subsystem be $c$ (Axiom~\ref{ax:budget}) and
suppose a subsystem translates at speed $v$. Writing the split as orthogonal
components, the internal rate $r$ satisfies $r^2 + v^2 = c^2$, hence
\[
  r \;=\; c\,\sqrt{1 - v^2/c^2},
\]
the standard dilation factor, with the photon limit $r = 0$ at $v = c$.
The orthogonal (Euclidean) composition is here an \emph{assumption}; the
question of why the emergent geometry of linearizations is Lorentzian
rather than Euclidean, that is, why straight worldlines \emph{maximize}
accumulated solving, is part of problem O1.
